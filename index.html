Hola! My name is Peter Musa, but online I go by Pe6tens, short for Persistence. I'm a digital artist and graphic designer from Jos, Nigeria, and I'm studying Computer Science at Miva Open University. I make illustrations, smudge portraits, vector vexel art, logos, cover art, animation and flyers. I've loved tech and creativity from a tender age, and I grew up playing video games and exploring tech with a curious mind. Now I'm learning to code, so I can build the things I used to only draw. Thanks for stopping by. Never bitter, never stalled, just digging deeper until there's room to flourish.
